# Privacy Policy for Scratch Now

**Effective date:** August 2, 2026  
**Contact:** kyossi212@gmail.com

Scratch Now (“the App”) is a macOS application that applies real-time scratch effects to system audio playing on your Mac.

## Data Collection

The App does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share personal data.

- No account or login is required.
- No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are used.
- No data is sent to our servers or to third parties.
- The App does not create or upload recordings of your audio.

## System Audio

To provide its core feature, the App may capture system audio on your Mac (with your permission via macOS).

- Captured audio is processed **only on your device**, in real time, to apply scratch effects and play the result.
- Audio is **not** saved to disk for later use, and is **not** transmitted off your device.
- You can revoke access at any time in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → System Audio Recording** (wording may vary by macOS version).

## Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
